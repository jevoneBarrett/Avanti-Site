## Exuma Cays full-day tour

8 hours on a private boat through the Exuma Cays, with six stops along the way.

1. ### Allan's Cay

   Feed the rock iguanas.
2. ### Norman's Cay

   See the Pablo Escobar sunken drug plane. Lunch (optional).
3. ### Compass Cay marina

   Meet the nurse sharks.
4. ### Big Major Cay

   The original swimming pigs.
5. ### Pipe Cay sandbar

   A beach stop or sandbar.
6. ### Staniel Cay Yacht Club

   Lunch.

## Rose Island tour

A private boat tour in two lengths: a **4 hour half day** or an **8 hour full day**.

1. ### Pigs

   Meet the swimming pigs in the shallows.
2. ### Sea turtles

   Slip into the water and snorkel with the turtles.
3. ### Rainbow Reef

   Snorkel a live fish reef.
4. ### Lunch of your choice

   At a beach bar or at the floating bar.

**Pickup:** Can be arranged at the boat marina by The New Sailors Choice restaurant or at the Atlantis marina.

## Eleuthera pink sand tour

An 8 hour private boat tour with four stops, finishing on the famous pink sand beach.

1. ### Meeks Patch

   Swimming pigs.
2. ### Egg Island sandbar

   Photos on the swings and time with the stingrays.
3. ### Harbour Island

   Explore the island by golf cart and visit the pink sand beach.
4. ### Sea turtles

   Feed the sea turtles.
5. ### Lunch (optional)

   At an available marina.

## How booking works

Simple and personal. You deal directly with us.

### Send a request

Tell us your date, group size and the tour you want.

### Get confirmation

We reply with availability and pricing.

### Set sail

Confirm your date. Pickup can be arranged for every tour.

### What to bring

- Swimwear
- Towels
- Sunscreen
- A camera

### Weather

If conditions are not safe on the water, we will work with you to reschedule.

### Questions

Call 1 (242) 445-7375 or email Bookings@avantioceancharters.com before you book.
